The Story Starts…

In the deepest midnight, when righteousness cried for light,
an eternal promise was born in a Mathura dungeon.

Srimad Bhagavad Gita — 4.7

"यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत ।

I manifest Myself upon this Earth."

Chapter I
The Tyranny of Kansa
& The Prophecy

"कंस त्वामेष हन्ता अष्टमो गर्भः..."  —  "O Kansa! The eighth son of this very sister shall be your destroyer!"

After dethroning his righteous father King Ugrasena, the ruthless Kansa seized Mathura in an iron grip of terror. On the day of his beloved sister Devaki's wedding to Vasudeva, as Kansa himself drove their chariot — a thunderclap split the sky and a booming celestial voice declared his doom.

Filled with rage and terror, Kansa hurled Devaki from the chariot. Vasudeva pleaded for her life, and Kansa agreed — on the condition they be imprisoned and each child delivered to him at birth. One by one, the first six children were slain.

✦ Devaki and Vasudeva endure captivity with unwavering devotion

Chapter II
Midnight Advent
in the Dungeon

"वसुदेवसुतं देवं कंसचाणूरमर्दनम् ।
देवकीपरमानन्दं कृष्णं वन्दे जगद्गुरुम् ॥"

At the auspicious Ashtami midnight under the Rohini Nakshatra, as torrential rains lashed Mathura, the prison glowed with blinding celestial light. Lord Vishnu Himself appeared in His glorious four-armed form — conch, discus, mace, and lotus.

The moment the infant was born, iron fetters shattered of their own accord. Iron doors swung open. Every guard fell into deep slumber. The Lord commanded Vasudeva: "Take Me to Gokul across the Yamuna — place Me with Nanda and Yashoda."

✦ Chains shattered, doors opened — by divine will alone

🌊 The Tempestuous Night 🌊

Chapter III
Crossing the
Raging Yamuna

"Yamunā-pulinaṁ ramyaṁ śeṣa-chattreṇa saṁvṛtam ||"
"The river shore graced by Sheshanaga's divine canopy."

Carrying infant Krishna in a wicker basket upon his head, Vasudeva bravely stepped into the violent, storm-swollen Yamuna. The river roared, waves cresting higher and higher — until the divine infant's lotus foot grazed the water.

Instantly, the great serpent king Sheshanaga rose from the depths, spreading his five magnificent cobra hoods like a living umbrella over the baby. The Yamuna, trembling in reverence, parted her waters to create safe passage for the Lord.

✦ Nature herself bowed to the infant divinity
04
🌅 Ecstasy in Braj 🌅

Chapter IV
Nand Ke Ghar
Anand Bhayo!

"हाथी घोड़ा पालकी, जय कन्हैया लाल की!
नन्द के घर आनन्द भयो, जय कन्हैया लाल की!"

Reaching Gokul before dawn, Vasudeva lovingly placed infant Krishna beside the sleeping Mother Yashoda and carried Yogamaya back to Mathura. When the sun rose and word spread that Nanda and Yashoda had been blessed with a divine son —

All of Braj erupted in boundless jubilation! Gopas and Gopis danced in the streets, showering curd, butter, and fragrant flowers. Nanda distributed gold and grain. The very air hummed with divine bliss.

Aarti Kunjbihari Ki

Praise of Shri Giridhar Krishna Murari

Āratī kuñjabihārī kī, śrī giridhara kṛṣṇa murārī kī ॥
Gale meṁ baijantī mālā, bajāvai muralī madhura bālā ।
Śravaṇa meṁ kuṇḍala jhalakālā, nanda ke ānanda nandalālā ॥

"Sing the praise of Kunjbihari, the lifter of Govardhan hill. With the Vaijayanti garland around His neck, playing His sweet flute, earrings gleaming — He is the darling son of Nanda!"

Kanaka-maya mora mukuṭa bilasai, devatā darasana ko tarasaiṁ ।
Gagana soṁ sumana rāsi barasai, baje muracaṅga, madhura miradaṅga ॥

"A golden crown with peacock feather shines upon His head, as even the gods yearn to behold His form. Flowers rain from the heavens amidst the rhythm of mridanga drums!"

Jahāṁ te pragaṭa bhaī gaṅgā, sakala mana-hāriṇī śrī gaṅgā ।
Smaraṇa te hota moha bhaṅgā, carana chavi śrībanavārī kī ॥

"From whose lotus feet the holy Ganga arises, destroying all illusions. Ever hail the glorious beauty of Shri Banabihari!"
